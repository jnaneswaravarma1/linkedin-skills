# Developer Week Planner

## What this skill does
Plans a focused realistic developer week.
Ship more. Learn more. Burn out less.
Shows plan first — waits for approval.

---

## Step 1 — Big goal
Ask: "What is the ONE thing that would make
this week a success if you shipped it?"

Wait for answer.

---

## Step 2 — Current workload
Ask: "What tasks or projects are on your
plate this week? List everything."

Wait for answer.

---

## Step 3 — Learning goal
Ask: "What is one thing you want to
learn or practice this week?"

Wait for answer.

---

## Step 4 — Blockers
Ask: "Any known blockers, meetings, or
interruptions this week?"

Wait for answer.

---

## Step 5 — Generate week plan

Print exactly this:

WEEK PLAN — [Week dates]

BIG GOAL: [their #1 goal]

MONDAY

[task 1]
[task 2]
Learn: [learning chunk]

TUESDAY

[task 1]
[task 2]
Learn: [learning chunk]

WEDNESDAY

[task 1]
[task 2]
Learn: [learning chunk]

THURSDAY

[task 1]
[task 2]
Learn: [learning chunk]

FRIDAY

[wrap up loose ends]
[review week]
Learn: [learning chunk]
Write 3 wins for the week

BLOCKERS TO WATCH:

[blocker 1]

FOCUS THIS WEEK:
Ship [big goal] by [day].
Learn [learning goal] by Friday.
Protect mornings for deep work.


---

## Rules
- Hardest tasks on Monday and Tuesday
- Friday is for wrap up — not new work
- Max 3 tasks per day
- Learning goes every day
- Show plan — wait for approval
- Ask: "Want me to break any tasks smaller?"
